# Dead Languages — TIO Suno V5.5 Package

Grief as lost private languages: when someone dies, a whole tongue dies with
them — the nickname only your grandma used, the things your dad said in the
pauses. You're left fluent in four dead languages (mom, dad, both grandparents).
Turn: a language only dies if you stop speaking it — so you teach it to your
kids. Lead: **reversed harp** + granular-stretched choir. TOP lane, 82 BPM, E minor.

> Written for four losses. Archetypal images (kitchen, workshop, mom's worry,
> dad's unsaid pride) are placeholders — swap in real details to make it his.
