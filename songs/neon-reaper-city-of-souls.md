# NEON REAPER (City Of Souls)

Cyberpunk glitch-phonk. A magenta grim reaper haunts a rain-slicked megacity,
harvesting souls as data — death reimagined as neon aesthetic and digital decay.

- **Model:** ACE-Step 1.5 (default). For deeper, more human menace, re-run with
  YuE (`vocal_priority: "premium"`).
- **Tempo / key:** 66 BPM, F# minor. Half-time, heavy, gradual downward pitch drift.
- **Feel:** anthemic, menacing, neon-drenched, corrupted.

## The hit hook
The `Neon reaper (reaper)…` call-and-response chant is the anchor — simple,
shoutable, sticks on one listen. New this version: a signature payoff line —
**"Reaper in RGB — I paint the city dead"** — and gamer-death imagery (`404 on
your future`, `press start to keep breathing… the continue screen is dead`) that
gives the hook a quotable identity beyond pure atmosphere.

**Why this version hits harder than the source draft**
- **A quotable payoff line.** The original hook repeated "nothing's what it
  seems"; this adds "Reaper in RGB — I paint the city dead" and "livin' in your
  head" so the chorus has a punchline that travels.
- **A pre-chorus that builds tension** ("the meter's in the red") so the hook
  lands as a release instead of arriving flat.
- **Sharper, more specific imagery** — swapped vague "collecting souls" filler
  for gamer/tech hooks (`404 on your future`, `press start… continue screen is
  dead`) that are instantly memorable and on-concept.
- **Kept the concept's signature moments** — the glitch breakdown, the 5-second
  silence, the devastating 808 drop, and the whispered "I'm always watching"
  outro — but tightened the wording so each hits cleaner.
