# Comfortable — TIO Suno V5.5 Package

Slow, heavy, hypnotic dark trap. Comfort as the most dangerous drug — the warm
recliner and steady check quietly killing the person you were becoming
(boiling-frog). Turn: rather be uncomfortable and awake than comfortable and
asleep. Signature lead: **hang drum** + sub-drop foghorn. 68 BPM, A minor.

> **Slow-verse build:** verses use short half-lines, ellipsis rests, `[Slow Flow]`
> / `[half-time, behind the beat]` tags, and NO `[double-time]` (house cadence-
> variance rule intentionally relaxed so the rap stays slow). Negatives include
> `no fast rap, no double-time` to guardrail Suno's word-cramming.
