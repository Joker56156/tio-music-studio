# Grave Shift — TIO Suno V5.5 Package

The graveyard shift as slow self-burial: clocking in while the kids sleep,
trading every sunrise for overtime, becoming a dad-shaped shadow — until you
punch out for good to be there when they wake. Double meaning (night shift /
digging your own grave). Lead: **bowed vibraphone**. TOP lane, 84 BPM, B minor.

