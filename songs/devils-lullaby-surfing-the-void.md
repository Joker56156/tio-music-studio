# Devil's Lullaby (Surfing The Void)

A dream-phonk lullaby-trap nightmare: the devil sings you to sleep in a mother's
voice while the undertow drags your consciousness into the void. Comfort and
terror wearing the same face.

- **Model:** ACE-Step 1.5 (default). For a richer, more human vocal, re-run with
  YuE (`vocal_priority: "premium"`).
- **Tempo:** 50 BPM (deep half-time drift — everything moves like slow water).
- **Feel:** hypnotic, narcotic, beautiful, wrong.

## The hit hook
The whole song hangs on a nursery-rhyme flip. **"Rock-a-bye"** gives the chorus
an instantly-familiar melodic gravity, and **"Hush little sinner, don't make a
sound"** twists the "Hush little baby" lullaby into the devil's promise. The
counting-sheep motif runs as a recurring `one… two…` whisper so the song has a
signature it can open, turn, and close on.

  lullaby-phonk feel before the atmospheric detail.
- Translated the concept's imagery into producible tags: "spiral eye bass" →
  `spiral-eye sub bass pulsing like a heartbeat`, "wireframe sheep" → `distorted
  wireframe-sheep percussion`, "chromatic aberration" → `chromatic-aberration
  shimmer`, "ASMR nightmare tingles" → `glitchy ASMR static`.
- Vocals stay mostly **soft and sung**; the demonic "screwed" tone is called for
  *occasionally* so it's a texture, not the whole take.

## Lyrics

```
[Intro]
[whispered] One... two... the sheep don't sleep
[whispered] Three... four... the void gets deep
Hush now
Hush now

[Verse 1]
Neon bleeds through my eyelids closed
Spiral eye blinks and the whole room glows
Wireframe sheep on a broken loop
Counting down to the vanishing groove
Cowbell rings like a funeral chime
Fifty to the beat, slowing down my time
Board on the black, I paddle out slow
Where the water's warm and the current's cold

[Pre-Chorus]
Feel it pull, feel it pull me under
No thunder — just the hum and the wonder

[Chorus]
Rock-a-bye, ride the tide
Devil's hummin' me a lullaby
Close your eyes, spiral wide
Surfin' the void, hypnotized
Down, down, undertow
Nobody hears you when you go
Hush little sinner, don't make a sound
Ride the wave till the world comes down

[Verse 2]
Pink and cyan in a fever haze
Static kiss on the back of my brain
Devil hums in a mother's tone
Sweetest voice I have ever known
Sheep fall backward off the edge of the moon
Sleep paralysis in a padded room
I should be scared but it feels like home
Prettiest ending I've ever known

[Chorus]
Rock-a-bye, ride the tide
Devil's hummin' me a lullaby
Close your eyes, spiral wide
Surfin' the void, hypnotized
Down, down, undertow
Nobody hears you when you go
Hush little sinner, don't make a sound
Ride the wave till the world comes down

[Bridge]
[screwed] Deeper... deeper...
The tide don't turn, the tide just takes
I count the sheep and the sheep count me
[whispered] Wake up, wake up — but you never wake

[Chorus]
Rock-a-bye... ride the tide
Devil's hummin' me a lullaby
Hush little sinner, don't make a sound
Ride the wave till the world comes down

[Outro]
[whispered] Five... six... you're under now
[fading] Devil's lullaby... surfin' the void...
Goodnight
```

**Why this version hits harder than the source draft**
- **One central hook instead of five competing ones.** The original cycled
  through many verses/choruses with overlapping images; this locks onto the
  "Rock-a-bye / Hush little sinner" hook and repeats it so it sticks.
- **A real narrative arc:** paddle out (V1) → surrender (chorus) → seduction
  (V2, "it feels like home") → the trap snaps shut (bridge, "you never wake") →
  stripped final chorus → fade under. The listener goes *somewhere*.
- **A signature motif** — the whispered counting-sheep `one… two…` — bookends the
  track (intro `one/two`, outro `five/six`) so it has an identity you remember.
- **Restraint on the horror.** The demonic screw is one deliberate moment in the
  bridge; the rest trades on *seductive* softness, which makes the menace land
  harder than wall-to-wall distortion would.
